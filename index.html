Next: B Machine

B

The machine

  1. 1 of 4

    Gantry

    Hiwin MGN15 rails on both long sides carry a 40 x 80 mm aluminum beam. The end plates are machined on the VF-3SS and bolt into the beam's tapped ends.

  2. 2 of 4

    Drive

    Gates GT2 9 mm belts and three closed-loop NEMA17 steppers. Closed loop means a missed step is caught instead of ruining the plot.

  3. 3 of 4

    Pen head

    A servo lifts the pen on a small MGN9 slide. When the pen is down, only the head's own weight presses it, so the pen pressure stays the same on every line.

  4. 4 of 4

    Bed

    A steel-faced MDF bed with rubber-coated magnets holds the sheet flat. Corner stops square the paper the same way every time.
