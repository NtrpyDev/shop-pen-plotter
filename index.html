    The machine

    D

    1. 1 of 4

      Gantry

      Hiwin MGN15 rails on both long sides carry a 40 x 80 aluminium beam. The end plates are Haas parts that bolt into the beam's tapped ends.

    2. 2 of 4

      Drive

      Gates 9 mm belts and three closed-loop NEMA17 steppers. Closed loop means a missed step is caught instead of ruining the plot.

    3. 3 of 4

      Pen head

      A servo lifts the pen on a small MGN9 slide. When the pen is down, only the head's own weight presses it, so the pen pressure stays the same on every line.

    4. 4 of 4

      Bed

      A steel-faced MDF bed with rubber-coated magnets holds the sheet flat. Corner stops square the paper the same way every time.

        Risks and how they are handled

        First step: the slide test

        Before the pen dock is cut, a small slide test checks the pen-lift slide. If it fails, the slide is redesigned before any metal is cut for the head.

        Checks already passed

          What could still go wrong

          • Seal drag on the pen slide is unpublished. Handled by the $59 slide test in the first order.
          • Three small items are not priced yet: M5 washers, 6 x 3 mm magnets and a rubber strip. They are not in the total.
          • Prices were read on 2026-10-05 and will move. Each stage is re-priced before it is ordered.
          • Shipping and sales tax are not in any total.
